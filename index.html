Andrej and Georgie host every show together, as equals, from opposite ends of the day. One of them is always up too late. The other is always up too early.

Andrej KneislVienna

Producer and DJ, known as DJ Andy'K. Andrej makes trance, progressive house and cinematic electronic music, from deeply personal albums to concept records like Opus No. 1: Vienna. On the show he brings the new releases, the studio stories and the questions nobody planned to ask.

“For me, music is more than passion. It's a friend.”

INSTAGRAM @DJANDYKOFFICIAL ↗DJANDYKOFFICIAL.COM ↗

Georgie WalkerMelbourne

Independent music promoter. Georgie connects emerging and established artists with listeners who genuinely care, through playlist curation, artist advocacy and communities across TikTok, Instagram, Threads and Facebook. Her world is Afro, organic, melodic, progressive, deep and tech house. On the show she brings the artists you haven't heard yet, and keeps the hosts on time. Mostly.

Real promotion. Real connections. Global exposure for independent artists.

A live music show, built like television.

DISTANCE // ZERO puts Vienna and Melbourne in the same virtual studio for new electronic music, live guests and the kind of conversation you don't get in a press junket.

Serious music.Serious production.Completely unserious hosts.

Electronic music // trance // progressive and melodic // new releases // artist discovery // DJs, producers, singers, songwriters and musicians // international guests // live performances and DJ sets // conversation // your questions, live.

The interviews are not standard PR. Before every show, Andrej and Georgie go through a guest's public stories, old interviews and social posts, then build the conversation around what they find.

Expect the question nobody has asked yet, and probably the photo the guest hoped everyone had forgotten.

      The association

      DISTANCE ZERO is being established as an international music and cultural association based in Vienna, Austria.

      Its purpose is to support international music and cultural exchange, connect artists and audiences across borders, promote emerging and established artists, and develop international music, broadcast, live-performance and cultural projects.

      DISTANCE // ZERO is its flagship international music format, connecting Vienna and Melbourne through music, artists, interviews, performances and live broadcasting.

      The association is also intended to develop a professional music and media production infrastructure and operational HQ in Vienna for international broadcasting, artist development, cultural projects and music production.
