# DISTANCE // ZERO — official production schedule (global rule)

The owner's rule of 2026-10-29, for every DISTANCE // ZERO project: Website, Virtual Space, Broadcast Studio,
Marketing Studio, Piano Show, DJ Show, Video Show, Podcast and the other formats, Show Planner, Training / Rehearsal
Mode, Content Calendar, automatic marketing planning, the backstage posters and programme displays, guest invitations.

**From January 2027 the regular shows take place once a month, always in the THIRD WEEK of the month.**

The official production week:

| Day | |
|---|---|
| Wednesday | REHEARSAL |
| Friday | FRIDAY SHOW (DJ Show) |
| Saturday | SATURDAY SHOW (Piano Sessions) |
| Sunday | SUNDAY SHOW (The Live Show) |

- Not weekly: all regular shows happen in one shared production week a month.
- ONE official calendar: every system uses the same source of confirmed dates. A change there must reach every
  connected system. Today that source is the website's published calendar, https://distancezeroshow.com/distance-zero.ics
  (read by the Virtual Space's backstage posters).
- The show names and their days stay as they are.
- No regular show is created automatically every week. Special events outside the regular programme are added
  separately, after approval.
- No invented times, guests or unconfirmed dates. Where the third calendar week is unclear, the concrete dates are
  confirmed by the owner before recurring events are created.
- Existing functions and confirmed one-off events (such as the opening show on Wednesday 27 January 2027) stay.
